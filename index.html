About us

At Humble Gardens, we believe exceptional landscapes require exceptional care. We are a high-end, full-service landscaping maintenance company built on a foundation of long-term client relationships and highly acclaimed references. Our long-term, deeply trained professional staff specializes in the fine details of estate care—offering irrigation testing and repairs, fertilization, pest control management, precise weeding, and full green waste hauling.

Whether you require daily, weekly, or monthly attention, we tailor our services to your schedule through flexible hourly billing or predictable monthly service contracts. Because we focus strictly on premium maintenance—stepping aside from brand-new installation projects or large-scale arborist work—our team remains entirely dedicated to preserving and perfecting the beauty of your existing landscape.
