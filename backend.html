Monthly invoices, step by step

  1. Each month, the system prepares a new invoice for every repeat client, copied from last month's.
  2. Crew hours from the timecards are filled in for that client, either billed or just shown as a record of the work, depending on how you charge that client.
  3. You check it, change anything, then send it (by email or on paper, to be agreed).
  4. When the client pays, you mark it cash, check or bank. The dashboard is updated.
